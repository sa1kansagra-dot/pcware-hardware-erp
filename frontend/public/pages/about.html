About PC Ware

PC Ware is Rajkot's leading specialized IT refurbishment facility, custom workstation builder, and component-level electronics testing center. Operating out of Suvarnabhumi Complex, Mota Mava, we bridge the gap between corporate enterprise hardware and cost-conscious Indian enterprises, gamers, and professionals.

Our 24-Point Quality Check Philosophy

Unlike informal second-hand markets, no product enters PC Ware store inventory until it successfully completes our 24-point diagnostic suite. We test thermals, run 30-minute Prime95/FurMark stress tests, verify every key on the keyboard, test display panels for dead pixels, inspect motherboard capacitors, and install genuine activated operating systems.

🔬 Dedicated Cleanroom Diagnostic Lab

Clean testing tables, anti-static grounding mats, and precision thermal repasting with Arctic MX-4.

🛡️ 12-Month Total Hardware Warranty

Every refurbished unit is serialized and backed by in-shop repair or immediate unit replacement.
